Privacy

What stays on your computer

Everything, unless you use the one optional feature below. Live Notebook has no server, no account and no analytics.

What is stored, and where

  • ~/.claude/live-notebook/notes/<Notebook>/*.md: your notes, with a small front matter block (title, notebook, type, tags, dates, and where it was saved from).
  • sources/: for a note saved from a Claude Code reply, the text exactly as first saved.
  • trash/: deleted notes, so you can put one back.

Set LIVE_NOTEBOOK_HOME to keep them elsewhere. Live Notebook does not keep the messages around a saved reply, nor your project's full path.

What leaves your computer

Only this, and only in Claude Code: to pick a note's type, a short title and tags, the plugin asks a small Claude model through your own Claude Code account. It sends a note's title and first 1,200 characters; for a notebook's tags, the titles and first 300 characters of up to 20 notes and the names of their project folders.

By default it does this only for notes saved from a Claude Code conversation, which Claude has already seen. Notes you write yourself on the web or in an editor are not sent. To change it, create ~/.claude/live-notebook/settings.json:

{ "ai": "off" }

"off" sends nothing, "chat-only" is the default, "all" also sends notes you wrote yourself.

The web notebook

It runs on your computer at 127.0.0.1. It refuses requests from other websites and from any address but its own, so a page you visit cannot read or plant notes. It ships its editor with it and loads nothing from the internet.

Local is not the whole story

Claude Code itself sends your conversation to Anthropic; that is how it works, and Live Notebook does not change it. What Live Notebook adds is a copy on your disk, not a copy on someone else's server.
